No miracles

Concept of God

Empirical science rests upon a quiet leap of faith: the assumption that the universe has always operated under uniform natural laws and will continue to do so tomorrow. Yet this is an assumption, not an empirical proof.

There is no scientific guarantee that physical constants were identical a billion years ago, or that gravity will behave the same way tomorrow morning. For all we know, the cosmos could have coalesced into its present configuration moments ago, creating the grand illusion of an eternal, orderly past. No scientist, no matter how brilliant, can guarantee with absolute certainty that nature will not suddenly rewrite its rules tomorrow. True existential certainty comes not from measurement, but from the unyielding reliability of the Creator Himself!

… He created everything and attached intrinsic measures (properties) with [precise] determination. [Quran 25:2]

Once God issues a decree, it does not change. Neither does anyone else has the power to change His Will. God is not a human we could watch Him do things. God’s Sunnah (behavior / way of doing things) is expressed through His creation.

… None can alter the words (decree) of Allah! … [Quran 6:34]

… No change is (possible) in the words of Allah … [Quran 10:64]

… There is no change of His words … [Quran 18:27]

… You will not find any change in the ‘Sunnah’ (way) of Allah … [Quran 33:62]

But, but, but …

What about the Will of God? If He authored the laws of physics, couldn’t He suspend them at any moment — performing miracles to demonstrate His absolute power and sovereign control?

If the cosmos had been fashioned by a lesser deity, one might expect him to prove his majesty through theatrical displays: cooling the sun on a whim, fracturing the moon in two, or causing a steel needle to hover on water. But such demands stem from a primitive human urge for spectacle.

If a person remains uninspired by the titanic swirl of spinning galaxies, the silent majesty of black holes, and the fiery birth of stars—or by the intricate dance of subatomic quarks and invisible forces—no parlor trick will ever convince them of God’s infinite Power!

The Creator’s grand architecture is anchored in absolute Wisdom. When He endowed creation with specific attributes, He did so with perfect foresight. He designed fire with the intrinsic property to burn; He has no need to revise His original blueprint by suddenly robbing fire of its nature. Only flawed human engineers must issue patches, updates, and mid-course corrections, because human knowledge is inherently incomplete.

Through His sovereign Free Will, God chose to actualize His decrees in precise, measurable proportions — what we call the laws of nature.

He established this unwavering structure so that humanity could observe, comprehend, and harness these reliable principles for the flourishing of civilization. While God possesses the absolute power to command anything into being, He intentionally willed that His Divine Command be realized through fixed, dependable laws.

… And Allah’s ‘Amr’ (Command / Will) has been firmly decreed [in fixed measurements]. [Quran 33:38]

Have you not seen that God causes water to fall from the height. Then, with it We produce fruit of different colors (kinds). And in the mountains are tracts white and red, of diverse colors (attributes), and raven-black. And of human beings, the crawling creatures and related animals, and the cattle have diverse colors (distinctive behavior). This is why of all His servants only those with knowledge (of nature) can get some idea of the glory of God (by examining the wonders in Nature) … [Quran 35:27-28]

No Miracles for the Messenger

They say, “If only a miracle could come down to him from his Lord! @{qxp| (then we would have believed in Him.) }@” Say, “God has the Power to send down a miracle, but most of them do not use their knowledge.” [Quran 6:37]

He does not wish to stun the human intellect with supernatural phenomena. He wants believers to reflect and use reason. See [Quran 12:108]

Those who deny the truth keep saying, “Why has no miracle ever been bestowed upon him from his Lord?” But you (O Prophet) are only a warner and to every community is a guide. [Quran 13:7]

And yet they say, “Why are not miracles bestowed upon him from his Lord?” Say, “These verses are in themselves miraculous signs concerning God and with Him are the miracles (that you can see in the Universe). As for me, I am but a plain warner @{qxp| (drawing your attention to His signs and to the consequences of your actions.) }@” [Quran 29:50]

Why! Is it not enough (of a miracle) for them that We have bestowed upon you this Book that is conveyed to them? For, herein is Grace, a Reminder, and it is a giver of eminence to people who accept it. [Quran 29:51]

(Though we did not, but) Even if We had sent down to you (O Prophet) a pre-printed book written on parchment (as a miracle), so that they could feel it with their hands, the rejecters would have said, ‘This is nothing but obvious trickery!’ [Quran 6:7]
